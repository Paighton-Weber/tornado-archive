Tornado Safety

Preparing for severe weather can save lives. Understanding what to do before, during, and after a tornado is essential for staying safe.

Before a Tornado

  • Monitor weather forecasts and alerts regularly.
  • Identify a safe shelter location in advance.
  • Create an emergency kit with food, water, flashlights, batteries, and first aid supplies.
  • Practice a tornado safety plan with family members.

During a Tornado

  • Move immediately to a basement or storm shelter if available.
  • If no basement is available, go to a small interior room on the lowest floor.
  • Stay away from windows, doors, and exterior walls.
  • Protect your head and neck using blankets, pillows, or a helmet.
  • Do not attempt to outrun a tornado in a vehicle unless absolutely necessary.

Review the National Weather Service guide explaining what to do during a tornado .

After a Tornado

  • Watch for hazards such as broken glass, exposed nails, and downed power lines.
  • Check yourself and others for injuries and provide first aid if needed.
  • Continue listening to local authorities for emergency information.
  • Avoid entering heavily damaged buildings until they have been declared safe.

Know the Difference

Tornado Watch

A tornado watch means that weather conditions are favorable for tornadoes to develop. Remain alert and be prepared to take shelter if necessary.

Tornado Warning

A tornado warning means that a tornado has been sighted or indicated by radar. Seek shelter immediately.
