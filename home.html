Understanding America's Most Powerful Storms

Tornadoes have shaped communities across the United States. This site explores historic tornadoes, how they form, where they happen, and how people can stay safe.

Website Purpose and Intended Audience

This website educates visitors about major U.S. tornadoes, tornado formation, and severe weather safety through maps, historical events, and preparedness information. This website is intended for students, teachers, weather enthusiasts, and anyone who wants to learn more about tornado history and preparedness in an easy-to-use format.

Tornado Safety Reminder

Know where to shelter before severe weather arrives. A basement or small interior room on the lowest floor is usually the safest place.

Did You Know?

Tornadoes have been recorded in all 50 states, although they are most common in the Great Plains region known as Tornado Alley.
