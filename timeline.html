Timeline of Major U.S. Tornadoes

Explore some of the most significant tornadoes in United States history, from the 1840 Natchez Tornado to the 2013 Moore Tornado.

1840 - Natchez Tornado

The Natchez Tornado struck Mississippi and Louisiana, becoming one of the deadliest tornadoes in U.S. history with an estimated 317 fatalities.

1925 - Tri-State Tornado

The Tri-State Tornado traveled 219 miles across Missouri, Illinois, and Indiana, killing 695 people.

1953 - Waco Tornado

An F5 tornado devastated downtown Waco, Texas, causing extensive damage and 114 fatalities.

2007 - Greensburg Tornado

An EF5 tornado nearly destroyed Greensburg, Kansas, damaging or destroying approximately 95% of the town.

2011 - Joplin Tornado

The EF5 tornado that struck Joplin, Missouri, caused catastrophic damage and killed 158 people.

2011 - Tuscaloosa-Birmingham Tornado

The Tuscaloosa-Birmingham EF4 tornado caused major destruction across Alabama during the 2011 Super Outbreak.

2013 - Moore Tornado

An EF5 tornado struck Moore, Oklahoma, causing widespread destruction and significant loss of life.
